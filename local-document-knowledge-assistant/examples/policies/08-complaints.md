# Complaints and escalation

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Response targets

Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days. A final complaint response is targeted within 15 business days. If more time is needed, support must send an update explaining the next review date.

## Priority

Suspected fraud and duplicate debit complaints receive urgent escalation to the operations queue. Support must preserve the customer’s description and transfer reference in the complaint record.
