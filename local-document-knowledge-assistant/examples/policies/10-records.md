# Records and receipts

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Receipts

A transfer receipt becomes available after dispatch. Receipts include the transfer reference, amount, fee, and destination country. A receipt confirms dispatch and does not prove that funds reached the recipient.

## History

Customers can export up to 12 months of transfer history from the app. Older history requests go to the records team. The records team responds within 7 business days.
