# Transfer delivery

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Delivery windows

Standard bank transfers arrive within 2 business days after verification is complete. Express transfers arrive within 4 business hours after verification is complete. Weekends and recipient-bank holidays do not count as business days.

## Delayed transfers

A transfer is delayed when its delivery window has passed. Support must check verification status, recipient details, and bank acknowledgement before opening a trace. A bank trace normally takes 3 business days. A pending status alone does not establish the reason for a delay.
