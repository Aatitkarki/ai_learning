# Transfer fees

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Standard fee

The standard transfer fee is 5 demo credits per transfer. The express delivery surcharge is 3 demo credits in addition to the standard fee. These fictional fees exclude any separately disclosed recipient-bank charges.

## Disclosure

The full fee must be displayed before the customer confirms a transfer. The quoted exchange rate is locked for 10 minutes. If the quote expires, a new quote must be confirmed. No production exchange rates are defined by this policy.
