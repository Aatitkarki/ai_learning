# Cancellation and refunds

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Eligibility

A transfer may be cancelled only before the recipient bank accepts it. Once the recipient bank accepts a transfer, support may request a recall, but recovery is not guaranteed. The customer must provide the transfer reference to request cancellation.

## Refund timing

An approved cancellation refund reaches the original funding source within 5 business days. Refunds cannot be redirected to a different account. The standard fee is refunded only when cancellation occurs before dispatch.
