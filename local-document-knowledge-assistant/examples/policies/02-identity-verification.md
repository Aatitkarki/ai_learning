# Identity verification

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Required evidence

Identity verification requires a valid government-issued photo ID and a selfie. Proof of address is required for transfers above 8,000 demo credits. The address document must be dated within the last 90 days.

## Review

Manual identity review takes up to 1 business day. Expired identity documents are rejected. Support may explain which document is missing but cannot approve verification or bypass the review.
