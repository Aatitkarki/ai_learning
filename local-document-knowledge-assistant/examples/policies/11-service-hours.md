## Chat support

Live chat support operates from 08:00 to 20:00 UTC, Monday through Saturday. Messages submitted outside these hours remain queued until the next support window.

The in-app account-lock function is available at all times. The fraud reporting form accepts reports 24 hours a day. A submitted report is not a guarantee of immediate human review.
