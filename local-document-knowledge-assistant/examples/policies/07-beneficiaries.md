# Recipient details

DEMONSTRATION DATA — fictional policies for software experiments only.

## Required fields

A bank recipient record requires the legal name, bank account identifier, and destination country. Support must ask the customer to confirm recipient details rather than guessing or silently correcting them.

## Corrections

Recipient details may be edited before dispatch. After dispatch, support must open a recipient-correction request. A correction request does not guarantee that the bank will accept the change.
